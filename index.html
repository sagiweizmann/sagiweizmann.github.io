About Me

I'm a Full Stack Developer with a B.Sc. in Software Engineering. I've been coding since the age of 8, driven by a passion for building things that make an impact.

Currently at iPostal1, I lead development on high-scale mail management systems using Node.js, Vue.js, Symfony, MySQL, and Docker. I thrive on solving complex problems and delivering production-ready solutions.
